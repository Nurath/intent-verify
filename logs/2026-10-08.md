## Changes shipped — 0.5.3

- `tools/validate_ledger.py`: `_UNSEEN_MARKS`, the marks that draw nothing
  even on a letter. `_one_line` drops them, and turns a character without a
  glyph into a space, before it puts the line together. `_seen_length` counts
  what a reader sees of a string (characters that show, the gaps between
  them, up to two marks on a letter), and `_too_short` takes the smaller of
  that count for a quote as written and as composed.
- No prompt and no format changed.

## Tests / verification

- 331 unit tests (322 at 0.5.2), hook selftest 35 of 35, mock benchmark
  exit 0. The new tests fail on 0.5.2 apart from the controls.
- Replay: all 180 recorded verdicts and all 194 stored stage-1 replies come
  out as they did.
- **Adversarial pass on the two fixes** (Sonnet 5.5; the fixes are by Opus
  5.5; 20 tool calls, 188,079 tokens), and this time the merge waited for it.
  The print fix held: 200,000 generated strings, and every code point placed
  in every field of manifests and ledgers in every mode, with no character
  that draws nothing on any line. No forged line, no crash, nothing more
  lenient than 0.5.2. What it found:
  - The quote fix was half a fix. Visible accents were counted without limit,
    so "a" and four of them was five characters and one glyph. A mark now
    counts only on a letter, and only the first two, as in what is printed.
  - Composing (NFC) takes eleven single characters apart into three, so one
    glyph counted as three. The length is the smaller of written and composed.
  - Eight more marks that draw nothing, the joiners and selectors of single
    scripts, judged by name and category. Added.
  - A dropped mark left a run of spaces, or a line with nothing on it. What
    draws nothing is now removed before white space and lines are handled.
  - Left as they are: private-use and too-new characters are not counted in a
    quote; a value that begins with a thousand unseen marks prints as a cut
    marker alone; the cost of measuring 10 MB values.
  Its reproductions all exit 1 now, its scan of every code point through every
  mode finds no bad line and no crash, and its comparison with 0.5.2 on 12,000
  fresh cases shows no change of exit code other than quotes that are now too
  short.
- Three of the tests written for these fixes had the wrong expectation and
  were corrected, not the code: two words are three letters once composed,
  and two letters with two accents are four characters by the rule.
