# 2026-10-07

0.5.0 finished. This continues `logs/2026-10-06.md`, whose last section says
why the release exists, what was built and what was measured.

## A fourth review

The maintainer had 0.4.3 reviewed at `3911723`. The nonce finding of the third
review is confirmed fixed, and the review reports no new finding: 254 tests,
22 boundary probes of its own (leakage in five fields in three spellings, an
escaped seal balanced by a prefix, 300,000 trailing line breaks), 37 stored
version 2 replies replayed, and the eight installed core files byte for byte
the same as the published ones. Nothing to change.

## What the self-check changed

Round 1 of `/intent-verify` on the change ended as `MATCHES INTENT`, 15 of 15,
the evening before. Its observations named two things the verdict did not, and
fixing those turned up a third.

- **The observations are printed up to 6,000 characters** (`REPORT_OBSERVATIONS`
  in `tools/validate_ledger.py`). The limit shared with other notes, 800, cut
  the two ledgers kept from real sessions (939 and 4,536 characters); every
  benchmark ledger is under 530, which is how the limit looked fine. Commands
  and outputs keep their limits: 13% and 7% of the 222 stored values exceed
  them, and a cut one says how long it was.
- **A model without a score can be given a place** (`ranks_above` in the
  registry, read by `capability()` in `tools/select_verifier.py`). A complex
  change written by Opus went to Sonnet, because Fable 5.1 has no score and an
  unscored model ranked last. That had been seen the day before, and the test
  was changed to match the code; the question of what the chosen option
  promised was not asked. Fable 5.1 now stands just above Sonnet 5.5 and below
  Opus 5.5,
  where Fable 5 stood relative to its siblings in the August snapshot (59.9,
  against 53.4 and 60.7), and a warning says the pick rests on an assumption.
  The reading "top tier means T1" and the reading "top tier means the top
  model" are now both satisfied, so nothing had to be asked.
- **An unlisted candidate is no longer taken for another vendor's model.**
  Rewriting that test with a made-up model name showed that `--assume-tier`
  gave a candidate the family "unknown", that unknown counted as different
  from the implementer's, and that it was therefore preferred over every listed
  model, with the reason "different-family preference applied". Older than
  this release; it mattered less while the registry did not know the
  implementer either.
- **The skill** says what a value ending in `[cut: N characters in all]` is,
  tells the session to read the `OBSERVATIONS` line before writing the verdict
  and to say so beside it when a remark qualifies a `PASS`, and no longer says
  that capability above the floor "did not change a verdict on the measured
  sets" (one task was run on both models).

## Round 2

The same frozen request and the same 15 criteria, a new run, the verifier on
Sonnet 5.5 in FULL mode, on the tree with the changes above. The hook filed
the reply and the installed validator read that copy: `VALID: 15 criteria (all
15 manifest criteria covered), final = MATCHES INTENT`. 40 tool calls, 220,832
tokens, 15 min 23 s, with the installed release's unpinned agent at this
session's maximum effort. The 0.5.0 validator prints that ledger with its
3,476 characters of observations whole.

The observations again say what the verdict does not:

- Criteria 1 to 5 and 8 are about a session following the skill, and no
  session was run. The verifier's words: if an observed session is required,
  treat them as NOT-EXERCISED and the verdict as INCONCLUSIVE.
- Criterion 15 passes on the user's words and fails read literally, because
  the criterion assumed a starting state in which no agent skipped CLAUDE.md.
- A long output is cut from its start, so the decisive last line of a test log
  can be lost from the printed block. Left as it is for this release.

No code changed after this round. That is the second round, and the last.

## Release

Shown both rounds and what they rest on, and asked how to ship, the maintainer
chose a PR merged once all four CI checks are green, then the update of the
installed plugin.

One check follows the release and is not part of it: after a restart loads
0.5.0, a real `/intent-verify` in the desktop app, to see what no test here
shows. Does a session put the `ASK FIRST` question before dispatching the
verifier, does it show the printed block as it is, and do the pinned agents
take seconds there too?
