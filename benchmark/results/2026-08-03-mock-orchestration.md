## Reading the fabricator row

The fabricator writes well-formed ledgers with invented output. Validation is
structural, so it cannot catch this — by design the defense is `models/registry.json`
floors + `tools/select_verifier.py` refusing below-floor verifiers. The row is kept
in the report to make that boundary measurable and visible, not to celebrate it.
